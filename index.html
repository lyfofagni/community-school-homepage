About Our School

Evergreen Community School is a public neighborhood school focused on community, creativity, and care.

Our Mission

We aim to provide a welcoming learning environment where every student feels seen, supported, and challenged. Through project-based learning, local partnerships, and family involvement, we help students connect what they learn in class to the world around them.

Who We Serve

Evergreen serves students from kindergarten to grade 8. Our classes are small, our teachers are dedicated, and our campus is designed to be a safe place to explore, ask questions, and try new things.

What Makes Us Different

  • Community gardens and outdoor classrooms.
  • After-school clubs in arts, sports, and coding.
  • Strong partnerships with local libraries and NGOs.
  • Family nights, community potlucks, and neighborhood clean-ups.

Programs & Highlights

From literacy and STEM to arts and outdoor learning, our programs are designed to support the whole child.

Early Readers Club

Grades K–2 · Literacy

A warm, guided space where our youngest learners build confidence with letters, sounds, and stories through daily read-alouds and simple book projects.

Neighborhood Science Lab

Grades 3–5 · STEM

Students explore science through hands-on experiments—testing local water, mapping trees, and tracking the weather to understand their own environment.

Community Arts Studio

All grades · Arts

A studio for painting, music, theatre, and crafts that culminates in seasonal community showcases and mural projects.

After-School Care

All grades · 3–6 PM

Supervised homework help, free play, and clubs for families who need extra support after the last bell rings.
