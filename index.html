About Our School

Evergreen Community School is a public neighborhood school focused on community, creativity, and care.

Our Mission

We aim to provide a welcoming learning environment where every student feels seen, supported, and challenged. Through project-based learning, local partnerships, and family involvement, we help students connect what they learn in class to the world around them.
